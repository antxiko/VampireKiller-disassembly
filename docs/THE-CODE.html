The code

Everything runs in the interrupt

INIT (0x4075) sets up the machine, hooks H.TIMI and leaves the Z80 on a jr $. From then on the whole game runs in p00:4028, once per frame:

  1. the Game Master's pause, if there is one (p00:40C5);
  2. the sound, with banks 14 and 15 in place;
  3. unless it is already inside (the semaphore at 0xC005), the state machine.

When it is done it puts back the banks kept at 0xF0F2 and 0xF0F3.

The dispatcher

p00:406B is Konami's dispatcher: the jump table sits right after the call. It pops the return address, which is the base of the table, and jumps to entry number A. The listing marks each of those tables as data and names their targets.

The game states

0xC000 is the state and 0xC001 the sub-state (table at 0x4161, p00:417D):

statewhat it is
0the intro (Konami's curtain)
1the title
2the demo
3a game starts
4a life starts
5playing
6Simon dies
7GAME OVER (with F5 CONTINUE if there is a Game Master)
8block cleared, after the boss
9room change
0x0Astage cleared (the door)
0x0Bthe pause (F1)
0x0Cthe shop
0x0Dthe Game Master's menu

The sub-state goes in B and each djnz jumps to the next one: the first thing after the first djnz is sub-state 1, and the last, 0.

Simon

0xC420 is Simon's state (p01:6B39): 0 standing or walking, 1 jumping, 2 crouching, 3 on the stairs, 4 falling, 5 hit (p01:6F85), 6 dies (p01:7093) and 7 goes through a passage: crouching on top of a passage and pressing jump, Simon goes to the room 0xC5B4 names (p01:70FB, p01:6DA9). The collisions with the background are at p01:7D68. The whip and the five weapons have their own records (0xC450 and 0xC460).

A room

On entering (state 9, p01:62F5):

  • the room comes from 0x6013 (bank 11) with the number at 0x6000[stage] plus the room's own: 48 blocks;
  • each block is 16 tiles (0x7EBB[stage], banks 11 to 13), and they are left in the buffer at 0xD100;
  • it is drawn from 0xD140 onto page 0: 22 rows of tiles, from y 32;
  • its script (0x9AB0) sets its palette and uploads its enemies' patterns;
  • p00:5B1B walks its secrets (0xE000): candles and blocks go to 0xC470; items, to 0xC500; vendors, to p02:9179;
  • its enemies are created (0x8668 in bank 14, p01:6181);
  • p01:6321 switches the screen on.

The enemies

Each enemy is a record in the list at 0xC800. When creating it, p00:5F48 gives it its sprites (0x6058 + type), its colours (0x6084) and its life (0x60E1). Every frame, its type's code (bank 3, through the dispatcher) moves it, and p01:6445 puts it together with the table at 0xB473: [dy][dx][pattern] per sprite. The bone dragon (0x0E) does not go through there: it places its sprites itself. Type 0x17 has no sprites.

What they throw goes in another list, 0xD700 (p02:9E5C), with its own types.

The sound

p00:509F takes the number in A: 0 silences everything, 0x80-0xFA is a tune (three PSG channels, table 0x8DC9 in bank 14: 16 tunes) and 1-0x7F an effect (29, at 0x8D8F). An effect only plays if its number is not lower than the one already playing (0xC096 acts as the priority). The score (p14:8BB4) has commands from 0xD0 up: 0xDx sets the tempo, 0xE0-0xE5 the octave…

How the listing is made

  • tools/bancos.py traces the whole cartridge keeping track of which bank is in each window; that gives the calls that cross the mapper (src/pNN.entries) and the dispatcher's tables (src/pNN.nocode).
  • tools/z80trace.py follows each bank's flow from those entries.
  • src/pNN.notes keeps the labels, the comments and the data ranges, anchored to addresses; tools/mkasm.py merges them with the trace and writes src/vampirekiller_pNN.asm.
  • make verify reassembles the sixteen banks with Pasmo and the whole ROM, and compares the sha256.

The density: 49.4% of the 14,502 instructions carry a comment, and all 1,909 routines are above 10% (make densidad).

Vampire Killer (Akumajō Dracula in Japan) was published by Konami for the MSX2 in 1986; its catalogue number is RC-744 and it is 128 KB. All rights in the game remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
