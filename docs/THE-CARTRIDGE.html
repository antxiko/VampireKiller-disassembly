The mapper

Bank 0 is fixed at 0x4000-0x5FFF. The other three windows are chosen by writing the bank number to 0x6000, 0x8000 and 0xA000. There is not a single write to 0x5000, 0x7000, 0x9000 or 0xB000, the registers of the mapper with SCC: that is why it is Konami4.

There are 39 writes to the mapper (tools/reconocimiento.py), and no bank shows up in two different windows. So each bank has one single address where it runs, and the listing can be split into sixteen modules, one per bank, each with its own org:

windowbanks
0x4000 (fixed)0
0x60001, 4, 11
0x80002, 5, 7, 9, 12, 14

They are loaded three at a time (ld a,N / inc a / inc a: 1-2-3, 4-5-6, 11-12-13) or two at a time (7-8, 9-10, 14-15). Each window's bank is kept at 0xF0F2 and 0xF0F3, and the interrupt puts it back when it is done (p00:4042).

The VRAM

The game runs in SCREEN 5: 256 pixels wide, 16 colours out of a palette of 512 (p00:4B61). Page 1 of the VRAM is the store: that is where the stage's 191 8 × 8 background tiles go when it starts, along with the 16 × 16 items (rows 0x50, 0x60 and 0x70) and the five 32 × 32 doors (row 0xA0). Page 0 is the screen: the room is drawn from the buffer at 0xD100, and the items, the doors and Dracula are copied from page 1 with the VDP commands (LMMM, HMMM, HMMC).

The sprites are mode 2 (16 × 16, coloured line by line). Since the VDP draws no more than eight on a line, p01:6560 rotates their order every frame when it copies them to the VRAM, so the flicker is shared out. So the VRAM table is almost never in the RAM's order (0xD400 and 0xD600), and Simon's check is done against the RAM.
