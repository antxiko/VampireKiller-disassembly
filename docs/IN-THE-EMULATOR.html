In the emulator

Everything drawn on this website is checked against openMSX on an MSX2 machine (C-BIOS_MSX2_EU, and C-BIOS_MSX2_JP for the Japanese title). The probes are Tcl scripts that put the cartridge where it is needed by poking the RAM, and dump the whole VRAM (128 KB), the palette and the VDP registers at the right moment.

omsx_habitacion.tcl starts a game, sets the stage at the ret of p00:44E8 (0xD000, 0xC411 and 0xD002) and the room just before p01:62F5 loads it (0xD001), and leaves Simon in mid-air in the middle of the screen. When p01:6321 switches the screen on it waits half a second and dumps. It is driven with environment variables:

VK_OUT=work/habitaciones VK_FASE=6 VK_HAB=1 \
openmsx -machine C-BIOS_MSX2_EU -cart vampirekiller.rom -script tools/omsx_habitacion.tcl

Tips for watching it

  • The stage is at 0xD000 (0 the courtyard, 1-18), the room at 0xD001 and the block of three stages at 0xD002.
  • Simon's life is 0xC415 (0x20, full).
  • With a Konami's Game Master in another slot, STOP freezes the game and ; steps one frame.
  • The sprite VRAM is no good for checking Simon: p01:6560 rotates their order every frame. Read the RAM instead, 0xD400 and 0xD600.

Vampire Killer (Akumajō Dracula in Japan) was published by Konami for the MSX2 in 1986; its catalogue number is RC-744 and it is 128 KB. All rights in the game remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
