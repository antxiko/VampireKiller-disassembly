Findings

What turned up when the cartridge was taken apart. Each item carries the address where it can be checked.

The locked doors are vendors, and they do not always sell

The castle's 16 white doors, and another 17 secrets hidden inside blocks, are 33 vendors (p02:9179). Each one sells one thing (table 0x5B0B), but what happens depends on their class (two bits of the secret) and on how many times you have hit them: the table at 0x9300 gives eight hits per class (p02:92BB). A class 3 vendor does not open the shop until the fifth hit; a class 2 one can take five hearts away; they all leave on the eighth. The whole table is in The game.

The books change the prices

With the white book the shop charges less, and with the red one more (0xC702, p02:9418): full life costs 15, 40 or 80 hearts depending on the book. Seen in openMSX; the whole table is in The game.

Dracula is a sprite and a VDP drawing

Dracula's head is a sprite, but the body does not fit: the cartridge saves the background on page 1 (0x80, 0x80) and copies the body from page 1 onto the screen with LMMM and transparency (p03:AD80, p03:AD93). When it changes frame, 0x5C is drawn on top of 0x5B without restoring the background. Checked down to zero in the four frames (coteja.py dracula).

Dracula's room comes out with a shifted row

The mirrored tiles of the portrait room go through p00:5969, which writes them to RAM row by row backwards from 0xE818, four bytes at a time. Row 0 lands at 0xE818 and row 7 at 0xE7FC, outside the tile: what gets uploaded is rows 6 to 0 and, at the bottom, the four bytes that were already at 0xE81C. The game draws it that way, and tools/graficos.py reproduces it that way; with that, the room checks down to zero.

A tile that comes from RAM

p00:54E4 uploads 640 bytes of tiles from 0xBDC8. The cartridge ends at 0xBFFF: the last 0x48 bytes are read from 0xC000 onwards, which is RAM. They go to page 1 of the VRAM (x 64-79, rows 0x77-0x7F), in the fifth tile of that batch. They do not come from the ROM, so the check leaves them out.

With a Game Master, another pause and a menu

The normal pause is F1 (state 0x0B). But the cartridge also looks in the four slots for the signature of Konami's Game Master (p00:5C92, at 0x7FFA) and records in 0xE600 whether it is there. Only with it:

  • STOP freezes the game from the interrupt, sound included (p00:40C5), and with that on the ; key steps one frame;
  • after GAME OVER, F5 CONTINUE shows up (p00:42EC);
  • from the title you get into its menu (state 0x0D), which picks the stage (p00:5E4B).

What Simon picks up: nearly all of it is a bit

Of the 25 items p02:8D3E handles (plus the five weapons, from 0x1A), many only set a bit or a counter: the ring (0xC434, which kills on touch while it lasts), the hourglass, the ribbon that lengthens the counters, the boots, the wings, the books, the staff. That is why their names in this listing are those of their drawing (row 0x50 or 0x60 of page 1), not of their effect.

The scroll (0x0F) gives three uses of the map (0xC70F): with F2 the play area is cleared and every room of the stage is drawn, with Simon and an arrow to the exit (p02:9552; the keys, at p00:4B9D).

Two exits with no way back that are not drops

Of the castle's 22 one-way exits (0xB9D3), all are drops or the way into the boss, except two: room 11 of stage 13 exits left to room 9, and room 10 of stage 17 to room 11, and neither comes back (coteja.py mapa).

The same frame as other Konami cartridges

The interrupt runs the whole game (p00:4028, through H.TIMI: INIT leaves the Z80 on a jr $), the dispatcher at 0x406B reads its jump table stuck right after the call, and each slot's bank is kept at 0xF0F2 and 0xF0F3 to put it back after the interrupt. What changes here is where each bank goes: 1, 4 and 11 always at 0x6000; 2, 5, 7, 9, 12 and 14 at 0x8000; and the rest at 0xA000. Not one is mapped in two places (39 writes to the mapper, tools/reconocimiento.py).

Vampire Killer (Akumajō Dracula in Japan) was published by Konami for the MSX2 in 1986; its catalogue number is RC-744 and it is 128 KB. All rights in the game remain with their holders. This is preservation, study and documentation work, and the cartridge image is not distributed.
